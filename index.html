Livia Betti

Hi! My name is Livia!

I’m a PhD student at CU Boulder studying statistical and geospatial machine learning. I am advised by Esther Rolf. As of Fall 2025, my work is supported by the NSF-GRFP.

Before coming to CU, I completed by undergraduate degree in mathematics at the University of Rochester, where I did research on VC-dimension under the mentorship of Alex Iosevich. I’ve changed paths quite a bit since starting graduate school, and I am currently working in the intersection of data-centric AI and machine learning theory for geospatial applications. In Dyson’s classification, I am a frog.

Outside of my academic life, I enjoy running, backpacking, and board games :)
